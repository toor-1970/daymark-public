Daymark

Privacy

12 November 2026

Daymark is a private mood log for one iPhone. The log stays on the phone. The passphrase is never sent. Daymark does not collect data.

On this phone

A mark is a mood, and sometimes one line. The moods are Heavy, Low, Steady, Clear, and Lifted. You can also note Alone, People, Work, Body, or Outside. The week is kept with those marks. All of it stays on the phone.

There is no account, no network, and no sync. Daymark does not ask for a name, an email address, contacts, or location.

The passphrase opens the log. It is never sent. Daymark does not store the passphrase. It is checked only on this phone, and it cannot be reset. Daymark cannot open a forgotten log.

"Stay unlocked on this phone" is optional. If you leave it on, Face ID can open the log on that phone. The passphrase is still never stored. Tap Lock and that opening is cleared. The encrypted log remains on the phone until you delete the app.

The bench

Ada, Nils, Mara, and Otto are on the phone. A brief you file for them stays on the phone. They never open the log. Nothing from the bench is sent.

Data

Daymark does not collect data. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third party in the log. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

Delete Daymark from the iPhone to remove the log from that phone. There is no separate account to close.

Not medical

Daymark keeps a private record of a mood. It does not diagnose, treat, or give advice.

Changes

If this page changes, the new text replaces this one. There is no account to notify.
